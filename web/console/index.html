/// THE FM-1'S STATE, 25 TIMES A SECOND · NOTHING IS WRITTEN TO IT

No FM-1 at hand?

The player is the firmware itself, compiled for the browser. Turn it on and play: the console above follows it as it follows an FM-1.

POWER ON starts it: the firmware loads (a third of a megabyte) and plays here.

Over USB the console only reads: the FM-1 sends what it is doing while this page asks, and stops when it is closed. It needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (Web MIDI with SysEx), as the installer does.
