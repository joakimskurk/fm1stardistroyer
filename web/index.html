A starship FM synth for the M-VAVE FM-1

Four banks of oscillators on an X/Y nav field · a morphing resonator · multiband destruction · delay and room

⚠ Not yet tried on a real FM-1. Star Distroyer is built and tested on a computer and in the FM-1 emulator, but no FM-1 has run it yet. Install it at your own risk, and back up your FM-1 first.

Testers wanted. Have an FM-1, and know how to get it back (below the Install button)? Be the first to play Star Distroyer on one, and tell how it went in an issue on GitHub: what worked, what did not, what the screen showed, and how it sounded.

Star Distroyer turns the FM-1 into one instrument with four engines in a row. A voice of sixteen oscillators, morphed across an X/Y field; a Reactor of four resonators that sweep through vowels, chords and combs; DESTROY, which splits the sound into four bands and wrecks each its own way; and SPACE, a delay feeding a room. Around them: a modulation matrix, four macros, a two-track step sequencer with segments and a Turing machine, and an arpeggiator.

4engines in a row: VOICE, REACTOR, DESTROY, SPACE

Hear it

DERELICT and RANGE are played the way one person plays one FM-1: one stock patch at a time, the app's own controls, the voice rules. DERELICT is four such passes, layered at fixed levels, never more than four at once; RANGE is one pass, with nothing mixed in. CINEMATIC layers eight parts as a multitrack, each a stock patch on the engine. All three are rendered on a computer by the firmware's own sound engine, the same C the FM-1 runs. The picture under each is its spectrum, low to high, from start to end: tap it to jump.

Install

  1. Back up your FM-1's flash first (M-Flasher or MvaveFM1Unbricker). Star Distroyer saves where the Felucca family keeps its data, and its first SAVE can overwrite another firmware's projects.
  2. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (they can talk to the FM-1 over USB MIDI).
  3. Connect the FM-1 directly to the computer with a USB data cable, and switch it on.
  4. Close other apps that use MIDI, tick the box, press Install and allow MIDI when the browser asks.

    in update mode: replug it and press Install again, and it finishes. If the FM-1 does not start at all, hold
    OCT− while switching it on: it starts in the USB rescue, where an installer always works. To go back, install

The sound

  

    
    
VOICE · four banks of four oscillators

      
    
        
  • Each oscillator is a harmonic 1–32 of the note with a fine tune, a carrier or a modulator.
    • 
        
  • XM sets the FM, WARP turns sine → triangle → saw → pulse, FOLD folds, DETUNE spreads.
    • 
        
  • The NAV X/Y blends the four banks; COURSE plays four stored X/Y cells from each note.
    • 
        
  • Harmonic generators re-roll a bank. MONO, DUO or POLY.
    • 
      

  

  

    
    
REACTOR · a quad morphing resonator

      
    
        
  • Four filters of one model: BP6, BP12, a variable BPF, KP combs, or two kinds of allpass.
    • 
        
  • Six stages hold every filter's pitch and level; POS moves through them, SLEW jumps or glides.
    • 
        
  • A stage clock and key tracking; HARM snaps the filters to the note's harmonics.
    • 
      

  

  

    
    
DESTROY · multiband destruction

      
    
        
  • Four bands, each with GAIN, BIAS, a shaper (SOFT HARD FOLD CRUSH), LEVEL and PAN.
    • 
        
  • PHAT lifts quiet parts and tails, SHARP spits transients.
    • 
        
  • A feedback loop with TAME to darken it, and a limiter so it never blows up.
    • 
      

  

  

    
    
SPACE · a delay into a room

      
    
        
  • Stereo or ping-pong, free (1 ms – 1 s) or on the beat, with feedback and tone.
    • 
        
  • The room: four diffusers into a four-line network, with SIZE, DECAY and TONE.
    • 
      

  

  

    
    
MOVEMENT · macros and the matrix

      
    
        
  • Four macros: MUTATE the voice, MOVE the nav and the Reactor, BREAK the destruction,
          MACRO 4 as the patch chooses. 0 is the patch as programmed.
    • 
        
  • Eight modulation slots: envelopes, two LFOs, the nav, velocity, the wheel, aftertouch, random, and the
          engines themselves (the step, the bands, the Reactor's stage, the echoes).
    • 
      

  

  

    
    
PLAYING IT · two tracks, a Turing machine, an arpeggiator

      
    
        
  • A melody on A and a bass line on B: up to 64 steps each, with slides, ties, ratchets, chance and chords.
    • 
        
  • Segments A–F cut a pattern on the white keys, each with its repeat and order.
    • 
        
  • TURING rewrites a share of the steps at each loop's one: the loop plays, the next is a variation.
    • 
        
  • The voices are shared by rules: no source cuts another's note.
    • 
      

  


  
The controls

  

    
HOMENAV → MACROS

    
FXREACTOR → DESTROY → SPACE

    
ENVENV → FILTER

    
LFOLFO 1 → LFO 2 → MOD

    
ARPthe arpeggiator

    
SEQSEQ A → SEQ B → TURING

    
K1–K4the four values in the row on top

    
SCL heldthe second layer (the names turn red); on SEQ and TURING, the keys transpose the track

    
ALGORITHMthe page's choice, named on the hint line; SCL + ALGORITHM the second

    
EDITON / OFF of what the page shows

    
PRESETSthe patch; on SEQ and TURING the pattern

    
SELECT · GLOthe tempo (40–240 BPM) · MONO → DUO → POLY

    
PLAY · REC · SAVEthe sequencer · recording · a place of your own
